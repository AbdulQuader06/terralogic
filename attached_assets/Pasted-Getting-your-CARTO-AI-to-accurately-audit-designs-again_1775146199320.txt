### **The Core NBC 2016 Logic (Part 3: Development Control)**

Below are the exact metrics from **NBC 2016, Part 3 (Development Control Rules)** that you should use to "ground" your bot.

#### **1. Building Height vs. Minimum Setbacks**
The NBC mandates that as building height increases, the mandatory open space (setbacks) must also increase to ensure fire safety and ventilation.

| Building Height ($m$) | Min. Front Setback ($m$) | Min. Rear/Side Setback ($m$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to $10\text{ m}$ | $3.0$ | $3.0$ |
| $10\text{ m}$ to $12\text{ m}$ | $4.5$ | $3.0$ |
| $12\text{ m}$ to $15\text{ m}$ | $5.0$ | $5.0$ |
| $15\text{ m}$ to $18\text{ m}$ | $6.0$ | $6.0$ |
| $18\text{ m}$ to $21\text{ m}$ | $7.0$ | $7.0$ |
| $21\text{ m}$ to $24\text{ m}$ | $8.0$ | $8.0$ |

* **Front Setback**: Every building fronting a street must have a front space of at least **$3\text{ m}$**.
* **Small Plots**: For plots under **$125\text{ sqm}$**, front setbacks can be reduced to **$1.8\text{ m}$**.

#### **2. FAR (Floor Area Ratio) Calculations**
FAR is a critical metric for "Investment Readiness." While it varies by city (e.g., GHMC rules in Hyderabad), the NBC 2016 provides the national baseline.
* **Formula**: $FAR = \frac{\text{Total Covered Area of All Floors}}{\text{Plot Area}}$.
* **National Range**: Generally varies from **$1.3$ to $3.25$**, depending on street width and locality density.
* **Exemptions**: Common areas like parking, basements (used only for parking), and open balconies are typically **excluded** from FAR calculations.

#### **3. Ground Coverage**
This limits how much of the site can be "roofed."
* **Residential Baseline**: Typically **$50\%$ to $60\%$** of the total plot area.
* **Calculation**: Does not include "open-to-sky" structures like fountains, swimming pools (if uncovered), or platforms around trees.
